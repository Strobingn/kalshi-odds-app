# Playbook: growth/offer

offer research → draft → review.

## Steps

1. **Planner** — define the offer's audience, promise, and done-criteria (promise matches researched demand, offer states price/value/risk-reversal, single CTA).
2. **Researcher** — research audience pains, competing offers, and price anchors; max 10 findings; tag confidence.
3. **Builder** — draft the offer copy: promise, proof, price, risk-reversal, CTA.
4. **Reviewer** — check promise against research; blocker for unsubstantiated proof; verdict.
5. **Router** — route per verdict; capture one learning line.
