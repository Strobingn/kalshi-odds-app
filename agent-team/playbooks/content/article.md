# Playbook: content/article

outline → research → draft → review.

## Steps

1. **Planner** — produce the outline: working title, thesis, section list with one-line purpose each, done-criteria (thesis proven, every section supported by research, consistent voice).
2. **Researcher** — per section, gather facts, examples, and sources; max 10 findings per step; mark unverified items.
3. **Builder** — write the full draft from the outline plus findings; complete draft, not a stub.
4. **Reviewer** — verify done-criteria; blocker for unsupported claims or structural gaps; verdict.
5. **Router** — route per verdict; capture one learning line.
