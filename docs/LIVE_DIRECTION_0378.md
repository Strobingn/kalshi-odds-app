# Kashi 0.3.78 — 55¢ ceiling and live opening direction

The algorithm buy ceiling is now 55¢ for both UP and DOWN, including delayed fills, maker bid prices, restored pending orders and every depth-walk level. New default V2 plans and the prior 50¢ shock presets use 55¢; deliberately cheaper presets, learned reductions and saved user limits remain lower. Global and per-strategy settings and the chart reference now allow/show 55¢. Existing held positions keep their saved stops and unrestricted exit prices.

Opening strategies observe from the first fresh quote at the start of each 15-minute window (entry window 0–30 seconds). Directional opening strategies compare the live YES mid with this window's initial fresh mid: a rise of at least 1¢ selects UP; a fall of at least 1¢ selects DOWN; no move means wait. V2 rechecks direction on the executable book after its two-second delay. If direction changes, the old order is recorded as unfilled and the source can signal the current side with a new order and delay. This also works without spot/strike/volatility data. Stale and sequence-gapped books cannot establish an opening baseline or fill V2 orders. Both-side and maker experiments retain their own intent.

Simple-dip strategies compare both eligible sides by their drop from their own rolling averages. They choose the stronger dip, wait on an equal signal, and exclude prices above the cap before choosing. Other strategies continue evaluating their own live forecast, fair-value, shock, momentum or dip rules; they are not forced to buy at the opening instant or to split orders evenly. History-dependent strategies still need their lookback.

Default V2 protection remains an immediate 3¢ bid-peak trailing stop plus a 20% fill-price stop, with the existing near-close exit policy. Learning and user overrides still apply. This is a paper experiment, not a promise of profitability.

Validation: Android unit suite covers mirrored UP/DOWN fills at 55¢ versus rejection at 56¢, per-level depth limits, lower saved limits, real opening signals before five seconds, reversal and re-signal with a new delay, stale/gapped opening quotes and unbiased dip ranking. Existing protective-exit/restart tests remain in the suite. APK build and signing verification run before updater publication.
