# Scalping KXBTC15M — rules, then ML — 2026-10-09

Question from Dirk: scalping is the only way to get an edge on Kalshi; get the
AI / ML to find it.

**Answer from this data: a scalp pays only from the front of the queue.**
Buying at the ask and selling at the bid loses 2–4¢ a round trip whatever the
signal, and a model cannot pick its way out of that. Resting in and resting
out earns a fraction of a cent, but only with fewer than about 250 contracts
ahead of the order; the usual queue is 2,000–10,000. A model that picks calm
moments roughly doubles that small edge. Nothing here was traded. Not
financial advice.

Scalp = open and close inside the same 15-minute window. Designs were written
down before scoring (`tools/research/scalp/DESIGN.md`). Intervals resample
whole UTC days.

## Costs

Taker fee `0.07 × P × (1 − P)` on the way in and again on the way out, plus a
1¢ spread: 4.5¢ per contract round trip at 40–60¢, 3.9¢ at 20–40¢ / 60–80¢,
2.5¢ nearer the ends. Resting orders pay no fee on this series.

## A. Minute-level scalps (4,264 windows, 2026-08-24 → 10-08)

Buy at the ask at minutes 2–11, sell at the bid 1–3 minutes later. Nine
signals (always the favourite, spot momentum 1 and 3 min, Kalshi momentum,
Kalshi fade, spot-model lag 3¢ / 5¢, spot jump follow / fade) × three holds.
In sample to 09-22, out of sample 09-23 → 10-08.

- All 27 cells lose out of sample: −1.6¢ to −4.3¢ per contract.
- Best in sample (spot-model lag ≥ 5¢, hold 3 min): **−3.23¢ out of sample**,
  95% [−4.14, −2.28], 3,935 trades.
- The same 39,074 favourite entries, two exits: held to settlement **−0.78¢**
  [−1.77, +0.15]; sold a minute later **−3.15¢** [−3.26, −3.02]. Selling early
  costs about 2.4¢ more than holding.

## B. Second-level scalps (1,992 windows, every public trade, 2026-09-18 → 10-09)

fills either only when a print trades *through* its price (back of the queue)
or on any print *at* its price (front of the queue). In sample 09-18 → 10-02,
out of sample 10-03 → 10-09. Signals: none, 10-second momentum, 10-second
fade, 30-second taker flow, flow fade.

picks above are 30 s cells or have a 30 s twin within 0.01¢.

## E. The model that ships in the app, and the fast strategies

**Compact model** (`train_compact.py` → `assets/scalper_model.json`). The app
can rebuild 23 of the features from live trade prints with a 60-second
lookback, so the shipped model uses only those. Same protocol. Threshold =
the validation cut with the highest total P&L (top 70%, "take everything the
model expects to pay"). Test days: its picks made **+0.35¢** per order at the
front of the queue, 99% [+0.24, +0.47], 7 of 7 days, against +0.18¢ for every
order; −2.02¢ at the back of the queue. `ScalperParityTest` replays a
recorded window in the app's code and checks all 23 features and every model
output against the Python values.

**Queue penalty** (`export_app_model.py`). Fitted on the first three test
days, the picks lose 0.23¢ with 100 contracts ahead, 0.46¢ at 250, 0.74¢ at
500, 1.13¢ at 1,000, 1.60¢ at 2,000. Taking only orders whose prediction
minus that penalty is at least zero, on the other four test days:
+0.16¢ per order at 100 ahead (30,872 orders) and +0.16¢ at 250 ahead
(12,051 orders), where taking every pick made 0.00¢ and −0.26¢. Past about
500 ahead that rule takes nothing.

**Fast strategies** (`strat_bt.py`, all 22 days, rules fixed before scoring):
dip-hunter (the side fell 2¢ or more in 10 s), momentum-sniper (rose 2¢ or
more in 10 s), extreme reversion (fell 6¢ or more in 30 s).

| Strategy | Buy at the ask, offer +2/+2/+3¢, stop 2/2/3¢, 30/30/60 s | Rest a bid, offer +1¢, stop 4¢, 30 s |
|---|---|---|
| Dip-hunter | −3.68¢ back of queue, −3.27¢ front, 0 of 22 days | −2.25¢ back, −0.00¢ front |
| Momentum-sniper | −3.49¢, −3.07¢, 0 of 22 days | −2.13¢ back, +0.06¢ front [+0.02, +0.11] |
| Extreme reversion | −3.67¢, −3.26¢, 0 of 22 days | −2.25¢ back, −0.01¢ front |

Each signal fires in about 40–60 of the 145 five-second slots per window and
side. Paying the ask on them lost on every one of the 22 days.

## F. In the app (1.8.4): the paper scalper

Home card **Paper scalper · aggressive · PAPER ONLY**. Seven strategies run
at once on the live Bitcoin window, 10 contracts an order, both sides, several
positions each, no caps: the ML scalper (resting), and dip-hunter,
momentum-sniper and extreme reversion each in a buy-now and a resting form.
The fast ones decide every 2 s, the ML scalper every 5 s as trained.

- Filled by real public trade prints. A resting order fills when a print
  trades through its price or when the queue ahead plus its own size has
  traded at its price. The queue is the size the Live signals book shows at
  that price (3,500 assumed when the book is not on). Nobody ahead cancels.
- Buy-now entries and stop / time-out exits pay the exact taker fee.
- The scoreboard shows every strategy with its history beside it, and for the
  ML scalper the split by queue (0–100, 101–250, 251–1,000, over 1,000) and
  the orders the queue penalty says should pay.
- It has no trade client and no credentials. Settings → Paper scalper turns
  it off and resets it.

What to expect from history: the buy-now rows lose about 3¢ a contract, the
resting rows sit near zero, and the ML scalper is positive only in the
0–100 and (queue-aware) 101–250 rows. The record on the phone is the first
test of any of it against real displayed queues.

## What this means

- A hand-approved order cannot scalp this market. By the time Approve is
  tapped the order is behind the queue, where every version loses.
- The only scalp with an edge is a resting one near the front, at about
  +0.2 to +0.4¢ per contract. At the app's $5 order (about 10 contracts) that
  is 2–4¢ a scalp.
- Getting near the front means posting the moment a price level opens and
  cancelling when the queue ahead grows or the model's calm reading ends.
  That is an automatic quoting loop, which the app does not have.
