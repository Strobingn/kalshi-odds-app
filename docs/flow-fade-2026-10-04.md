# Flow fade — Kalshi 15m BTC, 2026-10-04

Status: **buying at the ask on this signal failed its out-of-sample test and is
retired. A resting-bid version is being paper-tested. Nothing here is proven.**

Data: every public trade on 2,088 settled `KXBTC15M` windows, 2026-09-12 →
2026-10-04 (`tools/research/tape_study.py pull`). CIs are a market-cluster
bootstrap. Cents per contract, held to settlement.

## The signal

Every 15 s from 60 s to 870 s into the window: taker contracts in the last
30 s. Signal when total ≥ 500 and |yes − no| / (yes + no) ≥ 0.5, i.e. takers
are buying one side at least 3:1.

Buying **with** that flow loses: 4,474 triggers, 34.5% wins at a 37.3¢ ask,
−4.23¢ after the fee, 99% CI [−6.67, −1.69]. That part is solid.

## 1. Buying the other side at the ask: failed

Found on 2026-09-24 → 10-04 as the best of 18 order-flow rules, then tested
unchanged on the 12 days before. First trigger per market, asks 10–90¢, exact
taker fee.

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 09-24 → 10-04 (where it was found) | 751 | 63.4¢ | 67.8% | +2.94 | +1.96 | [−0.03, +6.05] |
| **09-12 → 09-23 (never seen before)** | 774 | 62.6¢ | 61.1% | **−2.93** | −3.92 | [−6.27, +0.26] |
| All 23 days | 1,525 | 63.0¢ | 64.4% | −0.04 | −1.02 | [−2.22, +2.15] |

3 of the 12 unseen days were positive. The first write-up of this file reported
+4.16¢ on 700 bets from the first 11 days; that was the luck of picking the best
of 18 rules. The other side does win a little more often than its price, but by
about the taker fee, so nothing is left.

## 2. Resting a bid on the other side: a lead, unproven

Same signal. Instead of paying the ask and the fee, join the best bid on the
side takers are **not** buying (their flow is what fills it). No maker fee on
this series. Cancel after 30 s. A fill needs Q contracts to trade at that price
first, or a print through it.

By half, signal side: 09-12 → 09-23 +0.77¢ (2,000 ahead) and −0.22¢ (5,000);
09-24 → 10-04 +2.72¢ and +2.13¢.

The signal moves a resting order by about +2.5¢ at every queue depth, which is
real. Whether that lands above zero depends on the queue: the best bid usually
shows a few thousand contracts, where the estimate is +1 to +2¢ with an
interval that includes zero, and the earlier half alone is flat.

## Limits

- Queue sizes are assumptions; cancels ahead of the order are ignored. Bid and
  ask are the last prints (≤ 10 s old), not the book.
- Two versions of one signal have now been tried on the same 23 days.
- A resting order needs a post-only live order path. The app has none; this is
  paper only.

## What the app does with it (1.3)

`FlowFadeRule.order` + `MakerPaperBook` post a resting **paper** bid on the
signal, using the size shown at the best bid as the queue ahead (3,500 if the
book has no size), and log it to the ledger only when later public trades fill
it (home card "Flow fade (resting bid) · PAPER", with a line for a 1.75% maker
fee). It never places an order. Needs Live signals on.

Bar before anyone bets on it: about 1,000 live paper fills with the P&L per
fill positive and its 99% interval above zero.
