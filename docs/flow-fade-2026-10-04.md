# Flow fade — a lead on Kalshi 15m BTC, 2026-10-04

Status: **a lead, not a proven edge. Paper only.**

## What was found

On the public trade tape (952 settled `KXBTC15M` windows, 2026-09-24 →
2026-10-04, same data as `docs/tape-study-2026-10-04.md`), 18 order-flow rules
were tried. One family stood out: when takers' buying over the last 30 seconds
is at least 3:1 on one side, the **other** side wins more often than its price.

Rule: every 15 s from 60 s to 870 s into the window, taker contracts in the
last 30 s; if total ≥ 500 and |yes − no| / (yes + no) ≥ 0.5, buy the side
takers are not buying, at its ask, hold to settlement. First trigger per
market. Asks 10–90¢. Exact taker fee.

| Rule | Bets | Avg ask | Win | Net ¢/contract | At +1¢ fill | 95% CI | 99% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| **Fade, first trigger per market** | 700 | 63.4¢ | 69.0% | **+4.16** | +3.18 | [+0.88, +7.40] | [−0.23, +8.28] |
| Follow the flow instead (every trigger) | 2,128 | 37.7¢ | 33.0% | −6.03 | −7.05 | [−8.59, −3.50] | [−9.28, −2.66] |
| Fade, threshold 0.4 | 894 | 61.4¢ | 64.9% | +1.99 | +1.01 | [−0.95, +5.04] | [−1.83, +6.04] |
| Fade, threshold 0.6 | 389 | 64.9¢ | 72.5% | +6.15 | +5.18 | [+2.23, +10.02] | [+0.71, +11.29] |
| Fade, entry 15 s late | 661 | 63.3¢ | 68.8% | +4.08 | +3.10 | [+0.74, +7.41] | [−0.31, +8.50] |
| Fade, entry 30 s late | 657 | 63.9¢ | 69.3% | +3.92 | +2.94 | [+0.53, +7.15] | [−0.43, +8.14] |
| Fade, first 7 days | 441 | 63.5¢ | 65.8% | +0.81 | −0.18 | [−3.35, +4.80] | [−4.68, +6.28] |
| Fade, last 4 days | 259 | 63.2¢ | 74.5% | +9.88 | +8.90 | [+4.79, +14.94] | [+3.32, +16.32] |

Positive on 8 of 11 days. Following the flow loses with a 99% CI well below
zero, so the signal itself (one-sided taker flow is wrong more often than
right) is the solid part. Whether fading it clears the fee is the open part.

## Why it is not proven

- It is the best of 18 rules on the same 11 days. With that many tries a 95%
  interval above zero is expected by luck; the 99% interval includes zero.
- Almost all of the profit is in the last 4 days (+9.9¢); the first 7 days are
  flat (+0.8¢). An edge that only shows in one stretch may be a regime or noise.
- The neighbouring thresholds do not line up (0.4: +2.0, 0.6: +6.2, 0.7: +1.2).
- The ask is a proxy from the last prints (≤ 10 s old), not the book, and
  depth at $5 is assumed.

## What the app does with it (1.3)

`FlowFadeRule` / `FlowWindow` run the same rule on the live WebSocket trades
and log **paper** bets to a ledger (home card "Flow fade · PAPER", with a +1¢
worse-fill line). It never places an order. Needs Live signals on.

Decision rule before anyone bets on it: at least 1,000 settled paper windows,
with the worse-fill P&L per bet positive and its 99% interval above zero.
